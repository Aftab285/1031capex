COMMON QUESTIONS

Helpful context before you begin.

What is a 1031 exchange? +

A 1031 exchange may allow an investor to reinvest proceeds from a qualifying property sale into replacement property.

What are the key deadlines? +

Timing is important. Contact the team early in your sale process to discuss your transaction.

When should I begin planning? +

Begin before your current property closes to give your exchange a strong foundation.
